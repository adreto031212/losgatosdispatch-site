This week in Los Gatos, Saratoga and Campbell.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Los Gatos Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Flooding in Saratoga: Wednesday's rain closed 10 Acres Road between Hill Top and Sobey roads. Check the alert page before you drive; never drive through standing water. In Los Gatos, report flooding or fallen trees at (408) 399-5770, weekdays 8 AM to 4 PM, or (408) 354-8600 after hours. Saratoga alert · Los Gatos storm line
  • Pollard Road at Highway 85 is closed to through traffic both ways since Wednesday, November 18, for urgent Caltrans maintenance; no reopening time given. Town of Los Gatos
  • The elevator at Hamilton light rail station in Campbell is out of service until further notice, VTA says (since November 17). VTA

What the city decided and what changed

Saratoga lights its holiday tree in Blaney Plaza on Friday, November 27, at 5:30 PM

The city's calendar lists the lighting from 5:30 to 6:30 PM in the plaza in the Historic Saratoga Village, as the start of the Village's holiday season.

Los Gatos and Monte Sereno want residents' answers on wildfire evacuation by December 12

The two are drafting an emergency evacuation plan with the planning consultant Dudek, and a short survey asks about evacuation needs and concerns.

Crews are clearing brush up to 20 feet from the edge of 31.09 miles of Los Gatos hillside roads
