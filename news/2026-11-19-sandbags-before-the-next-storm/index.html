Los Gatos, Saratoga and Campbell · One useful thing

sandbags before the next storm

November 19, 2026 · Los Gatos Dispatch, issue 1 · All Los Gatos, Saratoga and Campbell news

Both towns hand out sandbags for the winter. Los Gatos residents can fill up to 10 at the Balzer Field parking lot, off Miles Avenue just past the Los Gatos Creek Trail. Campbell's Service Center at 290 Dillon Avenue has bags to fill yourself, up to 25, Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 3 PM, plus a small supply of filled bags outside the gate on weekends for Campbell residents.
